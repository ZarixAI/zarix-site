Add-on service · Training & Courses

Your Team Is Already Using AI. Are They Using It Well?

Most teams have tried ChatGPT or similar tools on their own, with mixed results and no shared rules. Practical training turns scattered experiments into confident, safe everyday use, built around the work your team actually does.

The problem

Sound Familiar?

  • Some people use AI every day. Others won't touch it.

  • Nobody is sure what's safe to paste into it.

  • Tools get bought, tried once and forgotten.

What it is

Practical Workshops, Built Around Your Work

Not a lecture about the future of AI. Hands-on sessions on the tasks your team does every week, with the tools you already have, and simple ground rules everyone can follow.

What you receive

  • A workshop shaped around your teambuilt from your real tasks, not generic examples
  • Hands-on practicewith the tools you already pay for
  • Written ground rulesfor using AI safely at work
  • A take-away guideyour team can return to after the session

Topics

Three Workshop Topics

  • AI tools for everyday workWriting, summarising, research and planning, done faster and better.
  • Using AI safely at workWhat to share, what never to share, and how to check what AI gives you.
  • AI for your team's workflowsApplying AI to your own processes. Best after an AI Assessment.

Courses: short courses for owners and teams are in preparation. Ask about training and we'll let you know when the first dates open.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need technical staff?
No. The workshops are built for owners and everyday staff, with hands-on practice on real tasks.
Which tools do you cover?
The ones your team already has, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot or Gemini. We don't ask you to buy anything new to attend.
Do we need an AI Assessment first?
No. The first two topics stand on their own. The third, AI for your team's workflows, works best after an assessment.
Where and how long?
At your office or online, as a single session or a short series. Custom workshops are usually scheduled 3–4 weeks ahead.
What does it cost?
It is quoted per workshop, depending on the topic, format and team size.

Confidence spreads. Start with one good session.
