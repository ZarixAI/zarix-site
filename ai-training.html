Add-on service · Training & Courses

Your Team Is Already Using AI. Are They Using It Well?

Most teams have tried ChatGPT or similar tools on their own, with mixed results and no shared rules. Practical training turns scattered experiments into confident, safe everyday use, built around the work your team actually does.

Ask about trainingWhat to expect

The problem

Sound Familiar?

  • Some people use AI every day. Others won't touch it.

  • Nobody is sure what's safe to paste into it.

  • Tools get bought, tried once and forgotten.

What it is

Practical Workshops, Built Around Your Work

Not a lecture about the future of AI. Hands-on sessions on the tasks your team does every week, with the tools you already have, and simple ground rules everyone can follow.

What you receive

  • A workshop shaped around your teambuilt from your real tasks, not generic examples
  • Hands-on practicewith the tools you already pay for
  • Written ground rulesfor using AI safely at work
  • A take-away guideyour team can return to after the session

Programs

Training Programs

Every program is delivered in our own words and shaped around your team's real work. We keep them up to date as the tools change, and add new programs as they become useful.

  • Workshop · 90 minutes · or a 3-session series

    AI Tools for Everyday Work

    The main kinds of AI tools (writing and thinking, images, audio and voice, video, and tools that take action) and which ones fit your work. Hands-on practice, then an exercise mapping which of your team's tasks could be automated.

    You leave with: Your team's shortlist of tools and tasks to start with

  • Workshop · 90 minutes · or a 4-session series

    Prompting That Gets Results

    A simple structure for clear, reusable prompts, and the four levels of working with AI: casual chat, structured prompts, giving AI the right context, and building simple tools by describing them.

    You leave with: A shared prompt library for your team's real tasks

  • Workshop · 90 minutes

    Using AI Safely at Work

    What is safe to share and what never is, how to check what AI gives you, and simple ground rules everyone can follow. Built around your industry's privacy rules.

    You leave with: A short, plain-language AI use policy for your team

  • Facilitated session · 75–90 minutes

    AI Mindset for Leaders

    A guided diagnostic of how your company, teams and individuals approach change: openness, speed of learning, willingness to experiment, ownership and long-term thinking.

    You leave with: A clear picture of where to start, and where resistance will come from

  • Workshop · half day or full day · best after an assessment

    AI for Your Team's Workflows

    Your own processes, step by step: where AI helps, where simple automation is enough, and what stays with people.

    You leave with: An action list your team owns

Courses

Combine programs into a multi-week course

For teams that want to go further, we combine the programs above into a 4–8 week course, one session a week, with practice between sessions and a follow-up review at the end.

Examples

What Teams Do With AI After Training

Example applications, not client case studies

  • Restaurants and cafés

    Menu descriptions, social media posts, supplier emails and staff rotas, drafted in minutes.

  • Automotive

    Vehicle listings, follow-up emails after test drives and clear explanations of service work.

  • Retail, cosmetics and skincare

    Product descriptions, replies to customer questions and campaign ideas, in your brand's voice.

  • Clinics and wellness

    Admin only: appointment reminder wording, policies and staff guides. Never patient details in public AI tools.

  • Real estate

    Listing descriptions, market summaries for buyers and quick replies to enquiries.

  • Professional firms

    Meeting summaries, first drafts of proposals and research for client work, always checked by a person.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need technical staff?
No. The workshops are built for owners and everyday staff, with hands-on practice on real tasks.
Which tools do you cover?
The ones your team already has, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot or Gemini. We don't ask you to buy anything new to attend.
Do we need an AI Assessment first?
No. The first two topics stand on their own. The third, AI for your team's workflows, works best after an assessment.
Where and how long?
At your office or online. Most workshops run 90 minutes, as a single session or a short series; courses run 4–8 weeks, one session a week. Custom workshops are usually scheduled 3–4 weeks ahead.
What does it cost?
It is quoted per workshop, depending on the topic, format and team size.

Confidence spreads. Start with one good session.
