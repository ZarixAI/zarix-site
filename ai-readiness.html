What is the AI Assessment?

The AI Assessment is a short, fixed-scope review of how your business really runs. It shows where AI will pay off, what to leave alone and what to do first, before you spend money on tools. It takes about 2–3 hours of your team's time, and most assessments finish within two weeks.

The full assessment includes

  • Key Findings Reportwhere you stand today, your highest-value opportunities ranked, risks to manage, and an estimate of the time and cost being lost now
  • 30/60/90-day roadmapwith an owner and a clear “done when” for each step
  • One-page summaryfor your partners or team
  • Walkthrough callto go through the findings and agree your first step
