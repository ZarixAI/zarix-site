About Zarix AI

A Boutique Consultancy for Business-First AI

Zarix AI is a boutique AI consulting and agentic systems firm for business owners, headquartered in Toronto, Canada, with a regional office in Dubai, UAE. We help owners use AI where it pays off, and avoid it where it doesn't.

Zarix AI

AI STRATEGY & AGENTIC SYSTEMS CONSULTANCY

Zarix AI is a boutique AI consulting and agentic systems firm. We work as AI value architects: we design how AI fits the way your business creates value for its customers, so it grows without losing what makes customers choose you. Every engagement is led by our Principal Consultant, with more than 20 years of experience founding and running businesses across Canada and the UAE, in recruitment and talent, real estate and hotel apartments, and digital marketing, with a background in information technology and psychology, and certified in AI consulting and agentic systems.

That experience shapes how we work: technology only pays off when it fits how people actually work, so AI adoption succeeds or fails on people and process, not only on the tools.

Why Zarix AI exists

Most small and mid-sized businesses are being sold AI as a product. What they need is a clear view of where their time and money are lost, a better process, and only then the right technology.

Zarix AI was founded to provide that view. We work directly with owners and stay accountable for outcomes from first assessment to working system.

  • Value first. Process second. AI third.We start with what your customers value, map how the work really runs, and only then bring in AI.
  • Practical toolsSimple, maintainable systems over impressive ones.
  • Agentic systemsUsed where bounded autonomy adds real value.
  • Responsible adoptionPeople stay in control, with clear limits on data and access.
  • Measurable valueSuccess measures agreed up front and tracked after launch.
