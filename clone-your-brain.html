The problem

What it is

Everything Your Business Knows, in One Place Your Team Can Ask

A private, organised home for your processes, answers, policies, pricing rules and know-how, with an assistant your team can ask in plain language. It lives in your own accounts, and you own it.

What you receive

  • An organised knowledge basebuilt from your documents and conversations with your team
  • A private assistantthat answers from it, and says when it doesn't know
  • A simple routineto keep it current as your business changes
  • A short training sessionso your team knows how to ask it

What changes

What Changes for You

  • New hires productive faster
  • Consistent answers to customers
  • Every future AI tool works from your real knowledge, not guesses

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns it?
You do. It lives in your own accounts.
Is it secure?
Access is limited to your team. Sensitive records, such as patient, client or financial files, are never included without the right privacy agreements.
Does it work with ChatGPT or Claude?
Yes. It's built so the AI tools you already use can draw on it.
What do you need from us?
Your existing documents and a few conversations with the people who know the work.
What does it cost?
It depends on how much knowledge there is. We'll give you a fixed quote after a short call.
