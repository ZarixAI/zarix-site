Stage 1 · Where most clients start

AI Assessment

Find out where AI will pay off in your business, and where it won't, before you spend money on tools.

Who it's for

Owners who know AI could help but don't know where to start, or who tried tools that didn't stick.

Your time

About 2–3 hours across the team. Most assessments finish within two weeks.

What happens

A 30-minute kickoff call. Short, private 25-minute conversations with you and your key people; nothing is attributed to anyone. Then we study how work really flows: where it waits, where it depends on one person, and where the time goes.

What you receive

  • Key Findings Reportwhere you stand today, your highest-value opportunities ranked, risks to manage, and an estimate of the time and cost being lost now
  • 30/60/90-day roadmapwith an owner and a clear “done when” for each step
  • One-page summaryfor your partners or team
  • Walkthrough callto go through the findings and agree your first step

What changes

You stop guessing. You know where AI will pay off, what to leave alone, and your first step, before spending money on tools.

No commitment beyond this stage.

Stage 2 · Plan

Workflow Plan

Turn the opportunities you chose into a plan that can be built.

Interviews happen in short sessions of about 30 minutes, one workflow at a time, so they fit around your week.

Who it's for

Businesses that have chosen the areas to improve.

What happens

We start from what your customers value, the result and the experience, and work back to how the work gets done, mapping the chosen area step by step with the people who do the work.

Your time

A few hours of conversations with you and your key people. We do the mapping and the write-up.

What you receive

A build-ready plan showing what people keep, what software takes over, where a person approves, and how success will be measured. A visual map of how your business creates value, linked to the workflows we'll improve. A walkthrough meeting.

What changes

You know exactly what will be built and what it will cost before anything is built.

What your Workflow Plan answers

  1. 1

    What do your customers truly value?

  2. 2

    What does each part of your team need to deliver?

  3. 3

    Which workflows make that happen, and where do they leak?

  4. 4

    Who owns each one?

Only when these are clear do we decide what AI should do, and what should stay personal.
