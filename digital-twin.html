Add-on service · Digital Twin Videos

You Don't Need to Be Everywhere. Your Digital Twin Can Be.

How many times have you explained the same thing? What you do, how the first week works, what new staff should know. A digital twin is a video likeness of you, made from your own recordings, that delivers those messages in your own words, without another shoot.

Ask about digital twin videosWhat to expect

The problem

Sound Familiar?

  • You give the same welcome or explanation every week.

  • Your videos are out of date, and reshooting never makes the calendar.

  • New staff learn the basics from whoever happens to be free.

What it is

A Video Version of You, Made With Your Consent

We create your digital twin from a short recording session with you, then use it to make the videos you need, from scripts written with you and approved by you. When something changes, the script changes, and the video follows.

What you receive

  • Your digital twincreated from a short, guided recording session
  • A first set of videosscripts written with you, in your words
  • Your approval on every videonothing is used until you have seen it
  • Files you ownready for your website, onboarding and messages

Where it helps

Where a Digital Twin Earns Its Place

  • Welcoming new clientsA personal hello and what happens next, sent the moment they sign.
  • Explaining your servicesOn your website, in proposals or before a first meeting.
  • Onboarding new staffThe basics, in your voice, on day one.
  • Quick updatesChanges for your team or clients, without stepping in front of a camera again.

What to expect

How It Works

  1. 01

    Agree the videos

    Which messages, for whom, and where they will be used.

  2. 02

    Record once

    A short guided session for camera and voice, with a simple setup guide.

  3. 03

    Write and create

    We write the scripts with you and create the videos.

  4. 04

    Review and approve

    You approve every video. Updates are a script change, not a new shoot.

Quoted per project, agreed before we start.

Ground rules

Your Likeness, Your Rules

  • Only your own likenessCreated only with your written consent.
  • Clearly presented as AI-generatedWherever the video is used.
  • Never says what you haven't approvedNo new scripts without your sign-off.
  • Yours to keep, or to deleteYour recordings and likeness stay yours.

Examples

What It Can Look Like

Example applications, not client case studies

  • Holiday homes

    A short welcome for each property, sent with booking details.

  • Clinics

    "What to expect at your first visit", in the owner's own words.

  • Professional firms

    A one-minute service explainer for proposals and the website.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will people know it's AI?
Yes. Every video is clearly presented as AI-generated wherever it is used.
How much of my time does it take?
A short guided recording session at the start, then reviewing the scripts and videos. Updating a video later is a script change, not a new shoot.
Can it speak other languages?
Often, yes, depending on the tool. We will show you how it sounds before you decide to use it.
Who owns the videos and my likeness?
You do. Your recordings and videos stay yours, and you can ask for your likeness to be deleted.
What does it cost?
It is quoted per project, depending on how many videos you need.
