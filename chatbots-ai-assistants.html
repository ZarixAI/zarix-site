Add-on service · Chatbots & AI Assistants

What Happens to an Enquiry at 11 pm?

For most businesses, it waits until morning, and by then the customer has often asked someone else. A well-built assistant answers straight away, from your own information and in your tone, and hands anything that needs judgment to a person.

Ask about an assistantWhat to expect

The problem

Sound Familiar?

  • The same five questions arrive every day.

  • Enquiries that come in after hours wait until morning.

  • Your team stops what they're doing to answer the phone or WhatsApp.

What it is

An Assistant That Knows Your Business

One assistant, built for one clear job: answering customer enquiries, taking booking requests or helping your own team find answers. It works from the information you approve, speaks in your tone and knows when to pass a conversation to a person.

What you receive

  • An assistant built for one clear jobagreed with you before anything is built
  • Answers from your own informationservices, policies and the details you choose to share
  • Clear handoffs to a personfor anything that needs judgment, with the conversation attached
  • Set up in your own accountswith a short training session for your team

Two kinds

For Your Customers, or for Your Team

  • Customer assistantsAnswer questions, take booking requests and collect the details your team needs, on your website, WhatsApp or email.
  • Team assistantsHelp staff find the right policy, price rule or procedure in seconds, instead of interrupting the one person who knows.

What to expect

Ground rules

Built to Be Trusted

  • It says when it doesn't knowNo guessing, and no made-up answers.
  • A person approves what mattersQuotes, refunds and exceptions go to your team.
  • Always introduced as AICustomers can ask for a person at any time.
  • You own itEverything runs in your own accounts.

Examples

Where an Assistant Can Help

Example applications, not client case studies

  • Holiday homes

    Guest questions about check-in, parking and house rules, answered at any hour.

  • Clinics

    Booking questions, directions and what to bring. Never medical advice, and never patient records.

  • Home services

    Quote requests collected with photos and an address, then passed to your team.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?
Yes. The assistant always introduces itself as an AI assistant, and customers can ask for a person at any time.
What if it gets something wrong?
It answers only from the information you approve, says when it doesn't know, and hands anything uncertain to a person. You can see the conversations, and we adjust it in the first weeks.
Do we need an AI Assessment first?
No. Chatbots and AI assistants are available on their own. If several parts of the business need attention, an assessment first usually gives better value, and we will tell you if that's the case.
Where can it work?
Where your customers and team already are: your website, WhatsApp, email or your internal tools. We recommend the channel based on where enquiries actually arrive.
What does it cost?
It depends on the job and the channels. After a short call you receive a fixed quote before any work begins.
