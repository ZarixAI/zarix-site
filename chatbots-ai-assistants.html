Add-on service · Chatbots & AI Assistants

What Happens to an Enquiry at 11 pm?

For most businesses, it waits until morning, and by then the customer has often asked someone else. A well-built assistant answers straight away, from your own information and in your tone, and hands anything that needs judgment to a person.

Ask about an assistantWhat to expect

The problem

Sound Familiar?

  • The same five questions arrive every day.

  • Enquiries that come in after hours wait until morning.

  • Your team stops what they're doing to answer the phone or WhatsApp.

What it is

An Assistant That Knows Your Business

One assistant, built for one clear job: answering customer enquiries, taking booking requests or helping your own team find answers. It works from the information you approve, speaks in your tone and knows when to pass a conversation to a person.

What you receive

  • An assistant built for one clear jobagreed with you before anything is built
  • Answers from your own informationservices, policies and the details you choose to share
  • Clear handoffs to a personfor anything that needs judgment, with the conversation attached
  • Set up in your own accountswith a short training session for your team

AI sales assistant

An AI Sales Assistant That Never Lets a Lead Go Cold

Leads go cold fast. A sales assistant replies to every new enquiry within minutes, by phone, text or email. It holds a natural conversation, asks your qualifying questions and books the appointment. Then it hands the lead to your team with a full summary. With a voice assistant, it can also answer and make phone calls.

  • Always introduced as AIOn every call and message, from the first sentence.
  • Only people who asked to hear from youIt contacts leads who enquired or opted in, following the calling rules where you operate.
  • A person closes the salePrices, discounts and contracts stay with your team.
  • Every conversation on recordSummaries and recordings, where permitted, are saved to your own accounts.

Three kinds

For Your Customers, Your Sales or Your Team

  • Customer assistantsAnswer questions, take bookings and orders, and collect the details your team needs, on your website, WhatsApp or email.
  • Sales assistantsReply to new leads in minutes by phone, text or email, ask your qualifying questions, follow up when leads go quiet and book appointments. A person always closes the sale.
  • Team assistantsHelp staff find the right policy, price rule or procedure in seconds, instead of interrupting the one person who knows.

What to expect

How It Works

  1. 01

    Choose its first job

    We pick one clear job for the assistant to start with, such as answering enquiries or taking bookings, and agree what it must never do.

  2. 02

    Gather your information

    We collect your services, common questions, policies and the tone you want it to use.

  3. 03

    Test it with your team

    We try it on real questions with your team before any customer sees it.

  4. 04

    Go live, with a person in control

    You can see every conversation, and we fine-tune it during the first weeks.

Scope, timeline and a fixed quote are agreed before we start.

Ground rules

Built to Be Trusted

  • It says when it doesn't knowNo guessing, and no made-up answers.
  • A person approves what mattersQuotes, refunds and exceptions go to your team.
  • Always introduced as AICustomers can ask for a person at any time.
  • You own itEverything runs in your own accounts.

Examples

Where Businesses Use an Assistant

Example applications, not client case studies

  • Restaurants and cafés

    Takeaway orders, table bookings and menu questions, answered at any hour. Allergy questions always go to your staff.

  • Automotive

    Service bookings, test-drive requests and "is my car ready?" questions. Repair quotes always come from a technician.

  • Retail, cosmetics and skincare

    Product questions, order tracking and returns, in your brand's tone. Refunds are approved by a person.

  • Home services

    Quote requests collected with photos and an address, then passed to your team to price.

  • Holiday homes

    Guest questions about check-in, parking and house rules, answered at any hour.

  • Clinics and wellness

    Booking questions, directions and what to bring. Never medical advice, and never patient records.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?
Yes. The assistant always introduces itself as an AI assistant, and customers can ask for a person at any time.
What if it gets something wrong?
It answers only from the information you approve, says when it doesn't know, and hands anything uncertain to a person. You can see the conversations, and we adjust it in the first weeks.
Do we need an AI Assessment first?
No. Chatbots and AI assistants are available on their own. If several parts of the business need attention, an assessment first usually gives better value, and we will tell you if that's the case.
Where can it work?
Where your customers and team already are: your website, WhatsApp, email, phone calls with a voice assistant, or your internal tools. We recommend the channel based on where enquiries actually arrive.
What does it cost?
It depends on the job and the channels. After a short call you receive a fixed quote before any work begins.
