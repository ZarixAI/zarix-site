Privacy & Website Terms

Who we are

This website is operated by Zarix AI, a business registered in Ontario, Canada.

What we collect

If you use our contact form, we collect the details you enter: your name, email, business information and message. The 3-minute AI check runs in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you book a mini assessment and choose to keep your result attached to your enquiry; you can remove it before sending.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry and, where you have agreed, to follow up about our services. We do not sell personal information. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Website assistant

The assistant on this website answers only from published website content. In its current form, your questions are processed in your browser and are not stored or sent to us. Please don't type personal information into it.

Sensitive information

Please do not submit health, patient, customer or employee personal information through this website.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us through this website.

Website terms

Content on this website is general information, not professional advice for your specific situation. Examples describe possible applications of AI and are not client results.
